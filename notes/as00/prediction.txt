Prediction, before implementation

Expect 95 distinct mnemonic spellings and 280 mnemonic/operand-form pairs;
12 files using .macro or .irp. Expect the kernel to include rdtsc and
fxsave/fxrstor (state management), but no ordinary SIMD arithmetic.
Expect GNU as versus clang differences in zero-length section handling,
.note.GNU-stack, symbol order, and branch/operand-size encoding choices.
Expect under 5% of complete objdump instruction bodies to reassemble
without fixing symbolic operands, but over 80% of individual lines to parse.
Expect the first ten-instruction proof mismatch at mov immediate-to-r64:
C7 sign-extended imm32 versus B8 imm64. Recommend exact GNU as choice,
.code32 in ELF64 support, and GNU as as the baseline for every difference.

Implementation order: pinned harness and repo shape; full census; oracle
comparison and planted corruption; round-trip measurement; ten-instruction
table proof; empty ELF writer; README and next-lane contract in the PR.
