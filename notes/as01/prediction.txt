as01 prediction, before implementation

The first byte-identity failure will be a forward symbolic immediate in CMP:
resolving its eventual value too early would choose imm8 instead of gas's imm32.
Code-section alignment NOP fill is the next likely mismatch.
I predict 65 of the 189 source operand patterns need prefix, REX, addressing,
or code32 handling outside a selected encoding row. The table will be about
450 lines and the parser about 700 lines. The ISR --expand output will be
about 1,200 source statements plus their inline origin annotations.
The first full link will not be byte-identical, because symbol-table ordering
or alignment will reveal an object/layout assumption. After correcting those
findings I predict no final STOP rows among the 43 x86-64 inputs.
